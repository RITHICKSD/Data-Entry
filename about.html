Company Journey

The Evolution of DataX

From a boutique data entry task force to a global enterprise virtual workforce platform.

2020 • Founding

DataX Platform Launched

DataX was founded with a mission to eliminate manual administrative drag for fast-growing startups. Initial launch with 10 vetted spreadsheet specialists.

2022 • Automation Era

AI & OCR Pipeline Integration

Introduced automated OCR document parsing, catalog sync tools, and double-pass verification algorithms. Team scaled to 150+ dedicated processors.

2024 • Security & SLA

ISO 27001 Security & Real-Time Portal

Achieved ISO 27001 & SOC-2 compliance. Launched the DataX Command Portal with live hourly usage analytics and instant task submission.

2026 • Present Day

Global Enterprise Expansion

Now processing over 14,000,000+ data records monthly for 500+ global brands with guaranteed 99.9% accuracy and 24/7 coverage.
